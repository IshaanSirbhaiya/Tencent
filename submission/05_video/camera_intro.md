# Optional camera intro (about 15 seconds)

Recorded on a phone, facing the camera. It goes before the title card, and the narration then starts at
**0:06** in `narration_cues.md` (skip "Hi!" and "This is the Walnut Internal Brain…", because the intro
already says hello).

## What to say

> Hi, I'm Ishaan, from team Walnut. At a bank or a fintech, the answer you need is usually somewhere in
> Slack, Jira or a shared drive. But an AI that can read everything can also leak everything. So we built
> an Internal Brain that only ever answers from what *you're* allowed to see. Let me show you.

(About 45 words; at a relaxed pace that is 15 to 17 seconds.)

## How to record it

1. Phone held **horizontally**, at **eye level** (lean it on books), about an arm's length away.
2. Face a **window or a lamp** so your face is lit, not the background.
3. A quiet room and a plain background.
4. Look at the **camera lens**, not the screen. Relax, smile, speak a little slower than normal.
5. Record 2 or 3 takes; send the best one, or all of them.

Send it with the narration audio. Both get merged into the final video.
