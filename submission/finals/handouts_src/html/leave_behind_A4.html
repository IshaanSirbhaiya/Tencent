Answers you can prove.

At a regulated company, the same question must get different answers for different people, and nobody should be able to tell what they were not allowed to see.

What it does

  • Answers only from what the asker can open across Confluence, Jira, Slack and Drive, and cites the exact passage.
  • Two permission gates before the model, plus a re-check at the source just before release.
  • Keeps a sealed, hash-chained log of every request, so any change shows up.

Two proofs

Restricted looks exactly like non-existent. Ken asks about a real customer he may not see and about a company that does not exist: the two refusals are identical, byte for byte. 0 differences in 400 paired questions.
Tamper-evident audit. Change one decision in a copy of the log and Verify breaks at that exact entry.
