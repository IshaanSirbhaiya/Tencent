## The delete-detection problem, stated plainly

Four registrations present in the May capture are absent from the August
one. The API returns no tombstone, no `deleted_at`, and no change feed, so
**a deleted row and a row that fell outside the query are the same
observation**. That is why this source declares `delete_detectable: false`
in `../SOURCES.md`, and why an adapter that infers deletion from absence
is making up facts.
