# Regulatory mapping: MAS TRM, MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines, PDPA, PCI DSS, AML tipping-off

> **Read this first.** This page maps controls in the Walnut Internal Brain to regulatory expectations. It says
> where a mechanism **is aligned with** or **supports** an expectation, and what it does not do. It is not a
> compliance assessment, a legal opinion or a certification, and the Internal Brain runs on synthetic data only.
> Every row names its evidence (a test, an eval or a `file:line`) and its limits.
>
> `file:line` references are to commit `4d1887e`; paths are relative to
> `databridge/apps/internal_brain/databridge_ib/` unless they start with `tests/`
> (= `databridge/apps/internal_brain/tests/`) or `databridge/`. Regulatory paragraph numbers were checked against
> the official documents during research on 13 Oct 2026 (sources at the end). Wording is paraphrased.

## 2. MAS Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management (issued 7 Oct 2026)

Sections 3–4 apply from 7 Oct 2027 and Sections 5–6 by 7 Oct 2028, per the MAS release cited in our research.

| Para | Expectation (paraphrase) | Our mechanism | Evidence | Status | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **§4.7** | The AI inventory records attributes including the approved scope of use (e.g. jurisdiction), model type and data used | Every `answer.released` event records the model provider and id, whether a fallback was used, and the prompt version; sources and models are fixed in code and config | `pipeline.py:176-185`; `models.py:173-176`; `narrate.py:19`; `llm.py:24-25` | Provides inputs | **No** inventory record or card is generated. No jurisdiction scoping. Model ids are aliases (`hy3`), not pinned build versions. |
| **§5.4(d)–(e)** | Data classification; securing the inputs and outputs of AI | Inputs: only permitted, Gate-2-checked passages (P1). Outputs: grounding wall plus extractive fallback; answer text stored outside the ledger and revealed only with a logged reason. | `pipeline.py:155-185`; `audit/content.py`; `tests/test_audit_ledger.py::test_answer_text_never_in_ledger` | Supports | No classification labels; the FinCrime compartment (F1) uses source-native restrictions, not labels. Card masking at ingest (F4) is in progress. |
| **§5.9(c)** | Mechanisms to escalate AI outputs for human review when pre-defined conditions (e.g. on reliability) are met | Pre-defined conditions (a grounding violation, a model error, timeout or 429, an answer citing no released passage) automatically replace the model answer with a deterministic answer built from released sentences. Humans oversee through the audit console and the reasoned reveal. | `llm.py:65-81`; `pipeline.py:162-171`; `narrate.py:131-162`; `audit/routes.py:135-161` | Partially supports | The trigger leads to a **deterministic fallback, not a human review queue**. No near-miss log or reviewer workflow. |
| **§5.23(b)** | Incident management, including kill switches for AI | `IB_FORCE_OFFLINE=1` turns off every model call; the Brain then answers only extractively | `llm.py:55-57`; `bootstrap.py:67` | Partially supports | Global only and read at startup (needs a restart). No per-source, per-persona or per-compartment switch. |
| **§5.23(d)** | Access to AI systems and configuration controlled and logged; for genAI, consider logging prompts and responses with the applicable model versions | The ledger holds the question text, released document ids and versions, model id, prompt version and answer hash; the content store holds the answer text. Every audit access is ledgered. | `pipeline.py:105-185`; `audit/routes.py:79-90`; `tests/test_audit_routes.py::test_events_query_is_ledgered` | Aligned with | The exact prompt payload is **not stored verbatim**; it can be reconstructed from the released ids, versions and `PROMPT_VERSION` only while those document versions exist. Configuration changes are not ledgered. |

## 3. PDPA (Singapore)

| Section | Obligation (paraphrase) | Our mechanism | Evidence | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **s.24** Protection | Reasonable security arrangements against unauthorised access, use or disclosure | Two-gate access control before any model call; fail closed; a tamper-evident ledger to scope an incident ("who received document X") | P1/P3 evidence above; `tests/test_audit_query.py::test_doc_filter_and_accessors` | Synthetic data only. Supports the obligation; does not discharge it. |
| **s.26** Transfer limitation | Overseas transfer only with comparable protection | Per query, only permitted passages go to TokenHub's international endpoint (`llm.py:24`), recorded in our research as the Singapore (global) endpoint | DATA_FLOWS.md | Provider terms, retention and hosting location **not verified by us**. |
