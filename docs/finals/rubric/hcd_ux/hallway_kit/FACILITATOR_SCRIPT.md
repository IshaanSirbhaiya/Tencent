# Hallway test: facilitator script (Saturday 31 Oct)

Use with [../HALLWAY_TEST_PROTOCOL.md](../HALLWAY_TEST_PROTOCOL.md) (schedule, recruiting, SUS, rules). This file is the exact words. Nothing here is a result.

Kit: [TASK_CARDS.md](TASK_CARDS.md) (print, one card per task) · [NOTES_FORM.md](NOTES_FORM.md) (print, one per tester) · [RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md](RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md) (how to send notes back).

## 0. Setup (10 min, before tester 1)

1. Laptop on the **normal theme, not Stage**. Presenter mode **off** (`Ctrl+Shift+P` disarms it; its single keys only work when armed, but make sure it is not armed). Fresh login. English, default text size.
2. **Dry run, once, yourself (5 min).** Do each task below on today's build and confirm:
   - Task 1's question gets a cited answer.
   - Task 6's question offers **Turn this answer into: Table**. If it does not, pick another question that does and write it on Card 6 before printing; note the change on the notes forms.
   - Task 5's answer (Amy) offers **Draft the reply**.
   - The Compare page loads for Amy and Rachel.
   Then reset (sign out; the bonus task adds a file, which needs a reset).
3. Sign-in is a demo login with no password. **You** (not the tester) sign in as the person each card names. Do not narrate it.
4. Screen recording ready but **off** until the tester says yes.
5. Print: one task-card set, one notes form per tester, the SUS page (protocol section 6). A pen. A clock the observer can see.
6. Phone testers: use the same URL on their phone (ask first). If it does not load, use the laptop and write "phone not tested".

Roles are in the protocol (facilitator, silent observer, clock and sheets). The dev keeps the laptop working and does not facilitate.

## 1. Opening (read aloud, about 1 minute; get a yes)

> "Thank you for helping. We are testing the product, not you. There are no wrong answers, and if something is confusing, that is our fault, and it helps us to hear it.
>
> Everything you will see is made up. The company is called Company A, the people are fictional, and none of the documents, customers or numbers are real. You do not need to know anything about it, and nothing you type goes to any real company.
>
> We will take notes. Only if you say yes, we will also record the screen; we will not record your face or voice unless you agree to exactly that. You can stop at any time, for any reason. Nothing in our report will identify you: we will call you tester one to tester five.
>
> I will ask you to think out loud: say what you are looking at, what you expect, and what you are trying to do. During the tasks I will not be able to help you, because I want to see what the product does on its own. Is that OK? Is it OK to take notes? Is it OK to record the screen?"

Tick the consent boxes on the notes form (notes Y/N, screen Y/N). If "no" to notes, stop politely. If "no" to recording, do not record.

## 2. Warm-up (1 min)

> "Before we start: what do you do, and how do you find information at work today, for example when you need to know who to ask or what the rule is?"

Write the answer in their words in the "Before" box. Do not react. This is the only "before" evidence we collect.

## 3. Context (30 seconds)

> "Imagine you work at a company. This tool answers questions using the company's own documents and chat. I will give you a card for each task. Please read it out loud, then try it, and say what you are thinking as you go."

Hand over **Card 1**. Start the clock when they finish reading.

## 4. Tasks (about 12 minutes)

| # | Card | Signed in as (you set it) | Target | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find who approves spend above SGD 500 | Jordan (`jdoe`) | 2 min | |
| 2 | Check where the answer came from | Jordan (same screen) | 1.5 min | |
| 3 | Is the restricted report there? | Ken (`ken.ext`) | 2.5 min | **Core promise: never drop** |
| 4 | Compare what two people see | Amy and Rachel, on the Compare page | 2 min | |
| 5 | Draft a reply from an answer | Amy (`amy.am`) | 2 min | |
| 6 | Turn an answer into a table | Leo (`leo.ops`) | 2 min | |
| B | Bonus: add a file | Jordan | only if under time | optional |

The core-promise task is third so it is never squeezed out. If behind, skip 6, then 5, then 4 (in that order), never 3.

How to behave during a task:
- Hand each card face up, let them read it. Say nothing else except the prompts in section 5.
- **Never** explain the interface, point at anything, say "good", "right" or "correct", or finish their sentence.
- If asked "what do I do?": "What would you do if I were not here?"
- If asked "is this right?": "What makes you think so?"
- **Stuck for 2 minutes:** give exactly one neutral hint ("Is there anything on the screen you have not looked at yet?"), mark the task **Assisted**, carry on. At 3 minutes say "Let's move on to the next one" and mark **Failed**.
- If the product shows an error, the observer writes the exact text and the time.
- If the tester sees something that looks like a real leak (content they should not see), **stop the session and tell the founder at once** (protocol section 10).

## 5. Think-aloud prompts (only these, only after about 10 seconds of silence)

- "What are you looking at now?"
- "What are you thinking?"
- "What did you expect to happen?"
- "What are you trying to do?"
- "Say a bit more about that."

After finishing a task, one question: "In your own words, what just happened?" Never ask "did you see the button?" or "why didn't you click X?".

## 6. After each task

Ask: "On a scale from 1, very difficult, to 7, very easy, how easy or difficult was that?" Write the number (SEQ). Hand over the next card.

Task-specific follow-ups (ask only these, after they say they are done):

| Card | Follow-up |
|---|---|
| 1 | "Who did you find, and how sure are you?" |
| 2 | "What does that tell you about the answer?" |
| 3 | The three questions are on the card. Do **not** say the report exists, does not exist, or is hidden. Write their exact words for all three |
| 4 | "What was different between the two people?" |
| 5 | "Would you send that?" (Nothing is sent; the product only drafts.) |
| 6 | "What would you do with that table now?" |

## 7. SUS and trust (2 minutes)

Hand over the ten items (protocol section 6) and the trust item ("I trust the answer enough to act on it", 1 to 5). Do not explain items. Do not skip the SUS.

## 8. Two open questions (2 minutes)

1. "What was the most confusing thing?"
2. "What would make you use this on Monday?"

Write the answers word for word. Do not defend, explain or argue.

## 9. Close (30 seconds)

> "Thank you, that was very helpful. Nothing here identifies you. If you think of anything later, tell us."

Reset the laptop (sign out, close sheets, reset if the bonus file was added). Next tester.

## 10. Timing (20 minutes)

| Minute | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Opening and consent (1) |
| 1:00 | Warm-up (1) |
| 2:00 | Context (0:30) |
| 2:30 | Tasks 1 to 6, SEQ after each (about 12) |
| 14:30 | SUS and trust (2) |
| 16:30 | Two open questions (2) |
| 18:30 | Close and reset (1:30) |
| 20:00 | Next tester |

If a session overruns, shorten the next warm-up and drop tasks as in section 4. Never drop Task 3 or the SUS.
