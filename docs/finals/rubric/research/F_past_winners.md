## 2. Patterns shared by winners (10)

Each pattern is tied to evidence. Strength: S = strong (several winners), M = moderate, W = weak (one source).

1. **Social-good or accessibility problems.** Auntie AI (senior safety), EyeDentify (visual impairment), CalmGlow (autism), Jiaye (ADHD), Build for Good (waste, climate, hygiene), the 48h hackathon "AI for Social Good" theme. **S**
2. **Scam, fraud or safety.** Auntie AI, AI Scam Shield, Fraudbusters (finalist), Auntie AI again. **M**
3. **Problem statements from a sponsor or partner.** SingHacks had Julius Baer, Hedera, Ancileo/MSIG tracks; winners were grouped by track; the Demo Day was sponsor-led. **S**
4. **Agentic AI as the stated core.** SingHacks had all three tracks framed as agentic. AI Tinkerers required a working agent. UTM had an AI Agent track champion. **M**
5. **Compliance and regtech for banks.** JoTegNa and 4 Legs (RegTech). Matches MAS and bank-sponsored challenges. **M**
6. **Real-world enterprise readiness.** SingHacks aimed at "enterprise-ready" solutions (30+ produced). MAS Hackcelerator and SFF awards focused on deployable fintech (Fennech: treasury and payments orchestration). **M**
7. **Hardware plus AI for real users.** EyeDentify (low-cost wearable). **W** (single winner, but notable)
8. **Local language and culture.** Nativer (SpaceLingo), 2nd-tier Kepler prize. **W**
9. **Cross-institution teams.** AskNgo (NUS MSBA, NUS SoC and NTU) won the NUS-GURU challenge; Rep Monkeys and NTU SAARS on the same tracks. **M**
10. **Tiered, staged selection.** Regional rounds, then a global final (Tencent); finalists then grand final at SFF (Tenity); accelerator after a hackathon (Build for Good). Winners were the teams that advanced. **M**

Caveat: none of the sources gives scoring criteria, so these patterns describe who won, not why.

## 3. Patterns of teams that lost despite good tech (5)

I found **no source** that describes losing teams' tech, rejections, or judge feedback. The five patterns below are hypotheses, not findings. They are marked **[unverified]**.

1. **Generic chatbot with no named user.** [unverified] Not in any winner list; all winners named a user group (seniors, visually impaired, ADHD, nurses, travellers).
2. **Strong AI, no sponsor fit.** [unverified] SingHacks finalists such as The Trading Thing, ProvidAI and DINNR did not place, though their tracks were sponsor-defined.
3. **Demo without a clear business value.** [unverified] Tencent AI Tinkerers requires "a real business problem"; teams that did not show it may have lost.
4. **Tool use not visible.** [unverified] Tencent events required WorkBuddy or CodeBuddy; teams that did not use them visibly would be at risk.
5. **Overrun on scope in 48 hours.** [unverified] 48h hackathon: 17 teams made 17 projects. Teams that could not finish a working demo are not identified in the sources.

## 4. What Tencent-sponsored judges rewarded

Evidence is thin. Items marked [C] are stated in a source; others are inference.

- **Judges named.** SG 2026: Michael Xu (Chief Architect, Tencent Cloud), Feida Zhu (SMU), Terence (AI Singapore). [C]
- **Use of Tencent products.** AI Tinkerers: an agent must be built on WorkBuddy or CodeBuddy [S]. HK/Macau: CodeBuddy, WorkBuddy and Miora available to all [C]. Winners' use of these tools was not stated [NF]. Inference: a submission that is visibly built on the Tencent stack is expected, not sufficient.
- **Live demos.** Demo Day formats are confirmed for HK/Macau, UTM, SingHacks and MAS. Live versus video was not stated for any Tencent winner [NF]. Inference only.
- **Business value.** AI Tinkerers wording: "solves a real business problem" [S]. Auntie AI and AI Scam Shield both state an impact; the UTM track was "Silverlake challenge" [S].
- **Social good.** The 48h hackathon theme was social good, and most winners in Singapore and HK/Macau were accessibility or safety products [C] for the 2026 entries. Inference: social-impact framing is valued.
- **Global Finals.** Winners of each regional station advance to the 2026 Global Finals, Tencent Global Digital Ecosystem Summit, September. Results not found [NF].

Voice or agent demos: not stated for any winner [NF].
