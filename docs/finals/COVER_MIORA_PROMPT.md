## Main prompt (Night, for YouTube, the showcase and the dark product)

```
A premium 16:9 cover image (1920x1080) for "DataBridge by Walnut": a company brain for regulated
fintechs that answers questions only from what each person is allowed to see, and proves every answer.

Scene, a calm photographed still life on a dark charcoal-navy desk, softly lit from above:
- On the right two-thirds, six small piles of real paper records, slightly apart from each other: a
  ledger sheet with faint columns, an invoice, a payment-card statement with its numbers masked as
  dots, a signed contract, a small chat-note card, and a task-ticket card.
- From each pile ONE fine coloured thread (six muted, colour-blind-safe colours, one per pile: slate
  blue, sage green, warm ochre, dusty rose, teal, soft violet) runs toward the centre.
- Each thread passes through a tiny brass ROLE GATE (a small elegant gate or keyhole plate). Five gates
  are open and their threads continue; ONE gate is closed, and its thread stops cleanly at the closed
  gate.
- The five threads converge on ONE round glass LENS lying on the desk. Inside the lens they arrange
  into a tidy network that subtly forms the shape of a walnut / brain made of six parts.
- From the lens, one small clean answer card slides out, bearing a tiny citation tag "[1]" and a crisp
  green check mark.
- Behind the lens, softly out of focus, a neat stack of carbon-copy sheets joined by a fine thin chain,
  with a small wax-like seal: a sealed, append-only record.

Style: tactile editorial still life, like a Stripe or Linear brand illustration photographed for real:
paper texture, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, quiet and precise. Palette: dark background
(#0E1116 to #161B22), warm off-white paper, muted category colours on the threads, brass on the
gates, ONE accent: the green check. No neon, no glowing circuits, no gradients, no robots, no people,
no hands, no faces, no screens or UI, no real company logos.

Layout: the LEFT THIRD stays clean and dark, for text:
- small letter-spaced label "DATABRIDGE BY WALNUT" (muted grey)
- large title on two lines, "One connected brain" / "for your business" (bold, white, modern
  sans-serif like Inter, tight leading)
- one smaller line "Every answer cited. Every access checked. Every step on the record." (soft grey)
- a small rounded pill at the bottom left: "Tencent Cloud AI CAN DO IT · FinTech Finalist"
Spell every word exactly as written. Text must stay sharp and legible when the image is shrunk to
380x216.

Negative: misspelled or extra text, watermarks, real logos, cartoon or plastic 3D look, purple-pink
gradients, cyberpunk, sci-fi glow, busy background, more than one lens, more than one answer card,
people, hands.
```
