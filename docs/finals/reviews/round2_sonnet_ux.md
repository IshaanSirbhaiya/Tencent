## Verified good
- axe: zero violations (A/AA/2.2) on login, Ask home, the answer with Act row, Draft sheet and Ticket sheet in light and dark at 1280; Amy 390 answer; zh answer, draft sheet and composer; /present and /present/preflight at 1280 and 390. The only axe finding is #3.
- Keyboard: act buttons open with Enter; focus lands on Close in the sheet; Tab cycles inside the sheet; Esc closes and focus returns to the opener (act-draft and act-ticket both verified).
- Target sizes: act buttons 112x32 and 173x32, dictate 36x36, send 36x36, Create ticket 137x36 (all above 24 px).
- No horizontal overflow at 390 px on the answer view or the ticket sheet; the Act row wraps.
- Loading skeleton has a label; errors use `role="alert"` with retry; the created state moves focus to a status heading; Cancel says nothing was created.
- Confirm step: nothing is created until a separate explicit primary button; summary and project edits are visible first; the sheet says "in your name" and "recorded in the audit trail first". Unmistakable apart from #4.
- Rule 4 in the sheets: 404 and 409 each give one polite sentence with no reason; Amy's ticket sheet reveals nothing hidden.
- zh: every act.* and voice.* key exists in both languages; the composer, dictation status, Act row and sheet chrome are translated; `html lang` becomes zh-Hans; Esc stops dictation and keeps focus on the button; the status region announces "Listening" and "Stopped".
- Title slide copy: "Walnut DataBridge" / "One connected brain for your business." with a zh hook (brand stays English by design).
- Presenter bar: labelled section, step-number aria-label, polite announce region, status dots with text; Preflight rows use icon plus word plus colour.
