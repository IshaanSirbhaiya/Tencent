# Plan review (UX and demo focus), Sonnet

Scope: PLAN.md, DECISIONS.md, CLAUDE.md hard rules, research 02 and 04. Judged against a 5-minute Demo Day on Human-Centered Design, UX and Accessibility, Demo and Storytelling, and Overall Quality. Findings come from reading the plan and research. I did not open or run any code.

## Keep as is

1. D-07 and D-06: rejecting "N hidden" and keeping the trace to what the asker was already shown. This is the core proof.
2. D-08, no token streaming, with a staged status line. It is honest and fits the grounding wall.
3. Dark default with a restrained, single-accent system and no gradients or emoji.
4. Persona picker as sign-in, with a "can open" line per card.
5. Scope pill ("Answers only from what you can open") and the one-line privacy note.
6. `role="log"` that announces the complete answer once, and the reduced-motion handling.
7. `/audit/stats` computed on the server, with no browser aggregation, and compliance-only gating (D-05).
8. Backend items B1-B4 already done and tested; 645-test suite as the gate.
9. Compare page as the centrepiece "same question, two people" moment (restyled, not rebuilt).
10. Honest labelling of the fallback model and the replay history (D-10), plus the risk table's presenter reset and backup video.
