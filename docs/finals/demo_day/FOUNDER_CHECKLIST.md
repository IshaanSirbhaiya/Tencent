# Founder checklist for Demo Day (only you can do these)

| # | When | What | Why only you, and why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any time before Mon (2 min) | Double-click `Make Desktop Shortcuts.cmd` in `E:\Tenecent Hack_Walnut\Tencent\` once | It writes shortcuts onto **your** desktop. Agents don't change your desktop or system without asking. On stage you then double-click "Start Walnut Demo" instead of hunting for a script in a folder. |
| 2 | Night before (Mon 2 Nov, 10 min) | Apply the Windows settings in [DEMO_DAY_RUNBOOK.md](DEMO_DAY_RUNBOOK.md) §6 (list below) | These are **your laptop's system settings**. Changing them needs you, and the safety rules forbid agents from changing system settings. Each one prevents a known way live demos fail on stage (below). |
| 3 | At the venue, after plugging in the projector (2 min) | Check that the preflight board shows **Screen 1920×1080** and **100%**; press **F11** if the browser isn't full screen | Only the real projector shows its true resolution, and it differs at every venue. A wrong size makes text tiny or cut off for the judges. F11 is needed because full screen sometimes doesn't take when the browser opens. |

## The Windows settings (step 2) and what each one prevents

| Setting | What goes wrong without it |
|---|---|
| Do not disturb / Focus on (also while presenting or duplicating the display) | A WhatsApp, Teams or Windows pop-up appears on the projector mid-demo |
| App notifications off | The same, from apps that ignore Focus |
| Windows Update paused until after 3 Nov | A forced update or restart prompt during the slot (it happens) |
| Hotspot set as a metered connection | Windows starts big downloads on your hotspot and slows the Hy3 calls |
| Plugged in: never sleep; lid close does nothing | The screen sleeps or the laptop suspends while you talk |
| Display scale 100% at 1920×1080 | At 125–150% the app's layout and the Stage text sizes are off and text clips |
| Duplicate display (Win+P → Duplicate) | With "Extend", the demo opens on your laptop screen and the judges see an empty desktop |
| Time zone UTC+8 (Singapore) | Timestamps in answers and the audit log show the wrong time |
